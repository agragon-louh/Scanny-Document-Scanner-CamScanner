Scanny Privacy Policy

Effective 3 November 2026

Scanny is a document scanner that works entirely on your device. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no push notifications, and it does not have permission to use the internet. Nothing you scan is sent to us or to anyone else unless you choose to share it.

What the app stores

  • Scans, signatures and notes are kept in the app's private storage on your device. Other apps cannot read them.
  • Folder names, page names and settings (such as sort order and view mode) are kept in a database and preferences inside the app's private storage.
  • Temporary files used while sharing are kept in the app's private cache and deleted within 24 hours.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes all of this.

Files you export or share

When you save a PDF or an image, it is written to the Download/Scanny folder on your device. Files there are outside the app's private storage and can be read by other apps that you have allowed to access your files. When you share a scan, it goes only to the app you pick, and that app's own privacy policy applies from then on.

Password-protected PDFs are encrypted with AES-256. Use a strong password: the app cannot recover a forgotten password.

Permissions

  • Camera: to scan documents and read QR codes. Images are processed on the device.
  • Storage (Android 12 and older only): to save exports to the Download folder and to read files created by earlier versions of the app. Newer Android versions do not need it. Importing photos uses the system photo picker, which gives the app only the photos you select.

Text recognition

The "image to text" feature uses Google Play services' on-device text recognition. The image is processed on your device. Google Play services may download its recognition model the first time the feature is used; that download is handled by Google Play services under Google's Privacy Policy.

Backups

Scans are excluded from Google cloud backups. If you move to a new phone with a direct device-to-device transfer, Android may copy the app's data to the new phone.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of data changes, this policy will be updated in the app and the effective date above will change.
